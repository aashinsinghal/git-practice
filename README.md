# Elephants, Goldfish and the New Golden Age of Software Engineering

[Link](https://www.i-programmer.info/news/145-mapping-a-gis/18932-elephants-goldfish-and-the-new-golden-age-of-software-engineering.html)

I like the idea of using AI to question your ideas instead of just doing the work for you. When AI writes code, someone usually has to check it and fix it, which makes more work for other people. But when you use AI to push back on your thinking, you just end up with better ideas. This makes a lot of sense for product managers. A big part of their job is figuring out the problem and spotting issues before engineers start building. Having an AI poke holes in a plan helps with that, and it doesn't pile extra work on anyone else in the form of sprawling PRDs.

I also think the elephant and goldfish method works for way more than coding. The elephant is the chat where you build up all the details. The goldfish is a fresh chat that checks if your document makes sense to someone with no background. That idea works for school papers, business plans, or any big project with a lot of moving parts. It's really a test of whether you wrote things down clearly or just kept them in your head. For any task that needs a lot of context, this seems like the best way to use AI.
